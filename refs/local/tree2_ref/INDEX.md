# tree2_ref.png: 15 trees × 3 designs + dead + dead in winter

Source: `refs/local/tree2_ref.png`, 3374×1808: a screenshot of a pixel-art sheet at **~4.9×**
(re-grid period 4.898 × 4.892), blurry, on one flat dark-green background with labels
`DESIGN 1/2/3`, `DEAD`, `DEAD WINTER`. The 1× art was **reconstructed** by sampling the middle of
every art pixel; because of the blur the edges and small details are approximate (expect a few
stray tones). Each file is an exact copy of that reconstruction.
The user's private reference: study only, don't copy pixels.

Layout: `<row>/<species>.pxl`, one frame named after the row; rows `design_1 design_2 design_3
dead dead_winter`; species the same 15 as `../tree3_ref/INDEX.md` (oak, apple, pear, cherry,
birch, gnarled, sapling, wide_oak, orange, fir, spruce, cypress, palm, bamboo, willow) at the
same scale (18–42 wide, 26–61 tall).

## What it adds to tree3_ref
- **Three designs per species**: the same species redrawn with another crown shape, clump
  layout or palette (e.g. birch with yellow-green leaves, gnarled with a darker crown, bamboo
  on a dirt mound). Use them as variety: a forest mixes designs instead of repeating one tree.
- **dead**: no leaves. The trunk and roots are the living tree's; the crown is replaced by
  **branching**: 2–4 main limbs that fork into Y shapes, getting 1 px thin at the tips, lit on
  the upper-left edge. Palms keep dry drooping brown fronds, bamboo becomes dry gold canes,
  willow keeps thin hanging twigs, conifers become a straight pole with short stubs.
- **dead_winter**: the dead tree with **1-px snow on the top side of every branch** and a cap
  on the root tops.
