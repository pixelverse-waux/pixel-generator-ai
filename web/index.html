Pixel sprites

Hand-drawn sprites (32×32 cats and people, 48×48 dogs, top-down buildings), exported by python3 -m pixelkit.web and played by catsprite.js.

CatsWalk cycles, idles, sits, meows and more
DogsWalks, sits, pants, lying down and sleeping
CharactersPeople walking in four directions
TownA demo ground to walk around: grass, paths, fences, the house and trees
Buildings & treesTop-down houses and trees to walk around
