Town

A demo ground built from the hand-drawn 16×16 tiles in web/tiles/ (grass, dirt road, cobble, fences, flowers, pebbles and leaves) with the White House, oaks, pines, golden trees, bushes, and the cats and dogs wandering about. Click the map, then walk with the arrow keys or WASD: houses, fences, bushes and tree trunks block you, and you pass behind anything taller. Walk up into the White House's front door to go inside (furniture from web/interior/); the door mat takes you back out.
